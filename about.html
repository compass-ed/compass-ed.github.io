About

A campus, not just a homework site

Compass Ed is the online campus for your school: the things you can explore in your own time, the competitions worth entering, what's on this month, and the lessons and homework your teachers set. One account, one place.

For students

Explore activities in eight categories, from STEAM builds to one-line portraits. Enter competitions and challenges. Follow playlists your teachers line up, take quizzes with explanations, pass mastery checkpoints, and hand in work with drafts and feedback. A review queue brings back anything you got wrong until it sticks.

For teachers

A separate staff portal for writing activities, running competitions and judging entries, posting news and announcements with read receipts, managing the calendar, and the full teaching toolkit: units, lessons, quizzes, playlists, rubrics, assignments, marking and a class overview heatmap.

Privacy & safety

Built for a school

Your own accounts

Students need only a name, a username and a password. No email address is required. Accounts are created with a code the school hands out, so nobody outside the school can join.

Separate permissions

Students see their own class's work and their own progress. Teachers see their classes; an administrator manages people, classes and the join codes. The database checks this on every request.

Nothing public

Entries, submissions, grades, feedback and announcements are visible only to the student concerned and staff. Only categories, open competitions, whole-school events and news appear on the public home page.

Secure by default

Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes, sessions are random tokens that expire, sign-in attempts are rate-limited, and every page sets a strict content security policy.
