The rules of the campus

Plain-English terms for students, staff and parents. Using Compass Ed means you agree to them.

Last updated 28 October 2026 · version 1

Accounts and codes

  • Accounts are for people at the school. They are created with a login code, the school's join code or the staff invite code, or by a teacher.
  • Your login code and password are yours. Do not share them, and tell a teacher straight away if someone else has used them. A teacher can revoke a code and issue a new one at any time.
  • Use your real name so teachers know whose work they are looking at. Usernames must not impersonate anyone or contain offensive words.
  • One account per person. Do not sign in as someone else.

Using the site well

  • Be kind. Anything you write that others can see (competition entries, peer reviews, club sign-ups) follows the school's behaviour policy.
  • Do your own work. Hand in what you did yourself unless the task says otherwise; peer reviews are honest and constructive.
  • Do not try to break the site, guess other people's codes, overload it, or get access you have not been given. Repeated wrong sign-ins lock the connection for a minute.
  • Do not upload anything illegal, hurtful, private about someone else, or that you do not have the right to share.

Your work and content

You own the work you create. By handing it in or entering a competition you give the school permission to view, mark, store and, if you tick the gallery option, display it on the campus home page with your first name. Teachers may remove anything that breaks these terms. Content provided by the school (lessons, quizzes, activities) is for use within the campus.

Points, competitions and prizes

House points, badges, leaderboards and competition results are run by staff, whose decisions are final. Points can be deducted for late work or poor conduct. Prizes are the school's to award and are not an entitlement.

Linked services

Linking a Compass Dash account is optional and can be undone in Settings. Links to other services added by the school (for example Global Campus) take you to sites with their own terms. Events mirrored from an external calendar are shown as published by that calendar; Compass Ed cannot change them.

Staff

Teachers and administrators act on behalf of the school and must only use student data for teaching and safeguarding. The administrator is responsible for who holds staff accounts, for keeping the invite code private, and for revoking access when someone leaves.

Ending an account

The school can disable or delete an account when a student or member of staff leaves, or if these terms are broken. You can ask the school to delete your account at any time. Deleting an account removes everything it did.

Changes

If these terms change in a way that matters, you will be asked to accept the new version the next time you sign in. The date and version number at the top show the current one.

Limits

Compass Ed is provided as a school tool and may be unavailable at times, for example during maintenance or if the hosting services are down. Keep copies of important work. Nothing in these terms limits any rights you have under law.
