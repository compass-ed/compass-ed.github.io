Compass Ed: the school's online campus

What Compass Ed is

Compass Ed is the school's own website for lessons, homework, quizzes, activities, competitions, clubs, house points, the school calendar and announcements. Students sign in with a login code issued by the school, or a username and password, and see only their own class's work and whole-school items. The site is used for school purposes only.

What is stored about your child

  • Name, username, year group and class; a scrambled (hashed) password; the login code the school issues.
  • Work they do on the site: lessons and activities completed, quiz scores, homework hand-ins and feedback, competition entries, club sign-ups, house points and badges.
  • Photos or files they choose to attach to work or entries.
  • Announcements sent to them, and when they read them.
  • Optionally, a link to their Compass Dash account (a sister revision site) if they choose to connect it, which can be removed at any time.

The full list, who can see it and how long it is kept, is in the Privacy Policy at compass-ed.github.io/privacy.html. Data is stored in a database hosted by Supabase and is never used for advertising or sold.
