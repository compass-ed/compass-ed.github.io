# Compass Ed version history

Each new version from v2.2 onwards ships with an animated preview in `assets/versions/` and an entry on the site's Versions page.

## v3.5 — 9 November 2026
- **Compass Dash topics as homework.** Staff signed in to Compass Ed get a "Set as homework" button on every Compass Dash topic page: pick one of your classes and a due date. Students see it in Schoolwork with "Open in Compass Dash", which opens that topic. Their best topic-test score before the due date goes straight into the mark book, already marked; after the deadline only a first, late attempt counts. Teachers can still open a result to add feedback or change the score. Needs `supabase/compassed-v2.10.sql`.
- **A weekly summary for parents.** The private parent page can turn on a Friday note on that phone: work handed in, marks and their average, house points and what is due next week, in English, French or Arabic. It goes through the same `ed-remind` sender as deadline reminders (redeploy it after running the v2.10 SQL). When a child makes a new parent link, the old link's phones stop getting it. On iPhone, add the parent page to the Home Screen first; it opens the parent's own link from there. Signing out of a portal on the same phone, or switching one child's summary off, no longer stops the others.
- **Qatar History & Citizenship, Lessons 3 to 8 (Compass Dash v2.9.0).** Social change, social and economic security, sources of human rights, women's and children's rights, world peace, and intolerance and racial discrimination: each with a lesson, 15 questions, flashcards and two activities. All eight lessons can be read and practised in Arabic: switch Compass Dash to Arabic and the lessons, questions, cards and activities follow (typed answers are off in Arabic). Corrections to Lessons 1 and 2: the pearl trade collapsed in the late 1920s and 1930s, and oil had been found at Dukhan by 1940.
- **Phone header.** On the narrowest phones the signed-in header hides the logo text and trims the longest label, so nothing spills off screen, and the language menu stays on public pages after signing in.

## v3.4.4 — 9 November 2026
- **Polish pass on Compass Ed.** Card titles under a section heading are now `h3` (styled as before with `.as-h4`), and the Versions page entries are `h2` (`.as-h3`), so the heading outline no longer skips a level for screen readers. In dark mode, cyan text uses the brand cyan, which reads at 6:1 or better on every dark surface (the darker cyan was 3.3:1 on its own tint). Four-card rows go two by two between 520 and 1100 px instead of three and an orphan. The subject labels on the home page wheel and the small labels in its book never drop below 11 px. Version notes keep a readable line length.
- **Portal details.** A detail page such as a lesson shows its title in the top bar only once its own heading has scrolled away, so the title is no longer shown twice. The schoolwork board drops its coloured edge stripes; each card's badge already shows its state. Counting numbers use even-width digits so they do not jitter, text fields take the brand caret, scrollbars follow the theme, and gallery tiles without a photo get a tinted placeholder.

## v3.4.3 — 8 November 2026
- **Smoother Compass Dash (v2.8.8).** Movement is timed from the browser's frame clock (the timestamp `requestAnimationFrame` passes in) instead of when the code happened to run, so the runner moves the same distance every frame and stops stuttering. Subject rooms blend over a fixed time with an ease, and the crash freeze lasts the same time at 60 or 120 frames a second.
- **Higher frame rate on slower computers.** If frames keep arriving late (the middle frame time over 1.3 times the fastest one, checked every 120 frames), the background canvas steps down from 2x to 1.5x, 1.25x and 1x resolution until it keeps up. It never steps back up during a visit, and gaps from switching tabs are ignored.
- **Less work per frame.** The game cards on subject pages and the number chips in the portal headers no longer use a live blur over moving backgrounds, which the browser had to redraw on every frame. The trailer draws its dot grid once instead of every frame. The portal header aurora now animates at the screen's full rate, with its colour changes eased the same at any frame rate.

## v2.9 — 4 November 2026
- **Listen.** Lessons, activities, assignment instructions, news and announcements have a Listen button that reads them aloud with the device's own voices, in English, French or Arabic (the voice follows the language of the text, not the menu). Settings has a slower speed. Nothing is sent anywhere.

When a student says they are struggling or not great, or asks to talk, the administrators and the teachers an admin chooses get a notification, and see a follow-up list with each student's last seven answers.
- Fixes from reviews of each batch: read aloud keeps decimals and web addresses whole and no longer runs list items together; the speed buttons show the choice; a CSV fallback can't run formulas in Excel; slip counts only include students the slip still reaches; staff can only link events they can see; a parent changing just the note no longer re-notifies the student; printing the slip list and the portfolio no longer gives a blank page; wellbeing alerts go out once for a low mood and once for asking to talk (switching answers can't flood staff), again if it gets worse after a follow-up, and a later calmer answer can't remove the student from the follow-up list or erase their note; alerts disappear after six months and from staff who are no longer chosen; the six-goal limit can't be passed by reopening a goal; a wrongly pasted or mismatched reminder key stops the sender before it uses up any reminders, a push that fails for a passing reason is tried again the next hour, a moved deadline gets a new reminder and a reminder is dropped once its work is due; reminders follow a change of language without visiting Settings; offline copies of pages are cleared at sign-out; the year reads "2026/27" (a dash flipped it in Arabic); more labels and keyboard focus fixes for screen readers.

## v2.8 — 2 November 2026
- **The compass no longer spins at 1,000 turns a second on school computers.** Schools often switch Windows animations off, which tells browsers to reduce motion. The site's reduced-motion rule squeezed every animation to 0.001 s but let looping ones keep looping, so the home page compass (one slow turn every 80 s) spun about 1,000 times a second, and loading shimmers flickered. Under reduced motion every animation now plays once, so the compass stands still.
- **Videos fixed for school networks.** The trailer, the home and sign-in background loops and the v2.3 preview were exported from Moda as "fragmented" MP4s whose header gave a length of 0 seconds. On a slow connection the browser saw only the first chunk (about 3.4 seconds) and kept replaying it, so the trailer's spinning opening ran fast and never moved on. They are rewritten as plain MP4s with the index first and their real lengths (30.08, 12.14, 12.01 and 7.04 seconds); the frames are byte-for-byte the same. `tools/fix-video.py` does this for any new Moda export.
- **One sign-in for Compass Ed and Compass Dash.** Compass Dash lives on the same site, so it now reads the Compass Ed session itself (the old message between the two is gone). Students: opening Compass Dash signs them in to their own Compass Dash account, made for them the first time (named after their Compass Ed username, starting empty rather than with whatever a guest played on that computer); a student who already had one can switch to it in Settings and keep its progress, and Settings can also start a new account if a link ever breaks. Staff: after a Teacher Panel sign-in by hand inside Compass Ed, Compass Dash asks whether to remember it for that Compass Ed account; from then on it opens without a password (Settings shows the linked account, with Unlink). The link has its own session on the server, so signing out or locking the panel keeps it; it ends by itself if the seat is reset, disabled, its password is changed elsewhere or it is signed out everywhere in Compass Dash, and a seat another staff member holds is never taken over. Every "Sign in" or "Enter student code" button in Compass Dash now means the Compass Ed account; guests can still play without one, and a student code from a teacher still works ("Have a student code?").
- **Shared computers.** Whoever is signed in to Compass Ed decides who Compass Dash is signed in as: a new person never lands in the last person's account (the last person's unsent answers are saved to their own account first, and a "Signing in…" screen covers the switch), signing out of either signs out of both, and the staff 20-minute lock also locks the Teacher Panel (working in it counts as activity; Lock in the Teacher Panel holds). A student who declines Compass Dash's agreement is not asked again until they choose Sign in.
- **Full screen** button above Compass Dash in both portals; on iPhone, or where a browser refuses, Compass Dash fills the page until Exit.
- **A way back.** Compass Dash's top bar starts with "‹ Compass Ed" (and its Teacher Panel has a Compass Ed button): inside the portal it returns to the portal's Home and leaves full screen; opened on its own it goes to your portal, or the campus home when signed out. On narrow phones the Compass Dash title shrinks to its logo so the button keeps its label. The public pages' Compass Dash link opens it inside your portal when you are signed in.
- Fixes from a review of v2.7: the saved-progress import from the old address only accepts a visit from that address, only once, keeps the save that has answered more questions (with its own sync base) and forces its scores to numbers (a crafted link could otherwise plant script in shared storage); the forwarding page caps the whole address and keeps unsent answers ahead of other data; Compass Ed's and Compass Dash's offline copies no longer delete each other; Arabic all-day events no longer show "12:00 ص"; labels are no longer translated twice ("Rating" stayed "Note"), and text inside pictures is left alone.
- Database: `supabase/compassed-v2.8-one-login.sql` (run once after v2.7 and after Compass Dash's v2.8.0 teacher accounts). Tested in PGlite (real Postgres) against Compass Dash's own sign-in functions. Preview mode never signs sample accounts in to the real Compass Dash.
