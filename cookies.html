Cookie Policy

Cookies and browser storage

Compass Ed sets no advertising or analytics cookies. It uses your browser's own storage for a handful of essential things, listed here in full.

Last updated 28 October 2026 · version 1

What is stored, and why

NameKindWhat it doesHow long
ce_authlocalStorageYour session token and account details, so you stay signed in. Essential.Until you sign out; staff sessions lock after 20 minutes without use
ce_prefslocalStorageDisplay settings: light or dark theme, dyslexia-friendly reading, detail level, transparency.Until you change them or clear site data
ce_cookie_oklocalStorageRemembers that you have seen the cookie notice.Until cleared
ce_demo, ce_demo_v23localStorageOnly in preview mode: the sample campus kept in your browser.Until cleared or "Reset sample"
ce_cache_*sessionStorageA copy of pages you have opened, so they still show without a connection.Until the tab is closed
Service worker cacheCache storageThe app shell (styles, scripts, icons) for offline use and faster loading.Replaced when a new version is published

None of these are HTTP cookies, so they are never sent to a server automatically. The session token is sent only with requests to the school's database, over HTTPS.

Third parties

The pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Google receives the font request (your IP address and browser type) but sets no cookies through it. On compass-ed.vercel.app, Vercel's own page-view counter and page-speed measurement run; they set no cookies and are not tied to your account. No other third-party scripts, pixels or embeds are used. If a lesson includes a video, the video player loads only when you open that lesson and is governed by the video site's own policy.

Your choices

Because everything listed is essential to the site working, there is nothing to opt out of beyond not signing in. You can clear all of it at any time from your browser's site settings ("Clear site data"), which signs you out and resets your display settings. Signing out removes ce_auth immediately.
